Auto Reframe — Help

Basics

Open the comp you want to convert, click Refresh, pick the target formats, choose Fit All or Smart Stack, and click Generate Formats. Each format becomes its own new comp — the original is never touched.

Fit All vs. Smart Stack

Fit All scales the whole comp uniformly into the new frame via one controller null — fast, safe, zero setup. Smart Stack repositions layers by their Element Role (background, headline, lower-third, bug, OTS, foreground) into per-format safe zones — assign roles first for the best result; unassigned layers are treated as foreground.

Never baked, never shared

Expressions on your layers are left exactly as they are. Full-frame solids and (in Smart Stack with "Reframe inside precomps") nested precomps are duplicated per format before anything is changed — the broadcast master and any other comp using them is untouched.

Resync

Re-running Generate Formats with Resync checked finds the comp it previously generated for that exact source + format and reapplies any manual Offset/Scale Adjust nudges you made on its controller nulls, so re-running after a copy change doesn't erase your tweaks.
