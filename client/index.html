Motion Spell Checker — Help

Scanning

Pick a scope (Active Comp, Entire Project, Selected Layers, or Selected Comps) and click Scan — or open the gear icon for one-click "Scan Active Comp" / "Scan Selected Comps" buttons. The checker reads layer names, text layers, marker comments, string effect parameters, and quoted string literals inside expressions.

Word matching

Ignore ALL-CAPS, Skip words with numbers, and Smart word matching (also accepts plurals/tenses of a known word) are always visible in the toolbar.

Scan settings (gear icon)

Restrict which layers are scanned (hidden, locked, selected-only), and control on-canvas highlighting: "Force highlight visibility" temporarily shows hidden layers that contain errors, and "Disable global highlights (faster)" skips drawing highlights entirely for large projects.

Highlighting in the comp

After a scan, every layer with a spelling error gets a red outline directly in the Composition viewer, drawn on a non-rendering guide layer named "MSC Highlights" — it never modifies your actual layers and is excluded from render. Toggle it on/off with the eye icon, or clear it with Clear.

Fixing a word

Select a misspelled word on the left, review where it appears, then either pick a suggestion and click Replace (fixes every occurrence of that word), click Ignore (skips it for this session and saves it to ignoredWords.txt), or click Add to Dictionary to whitelist it permanently.

Dictionary files

Drop category word lists (one word per line, or wrong -> right correction lines) into the extension's Dictionary folder for full coverage beyond the built-in fallback list. Use Verify Dictionary to see what's currently loaded.
