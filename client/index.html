Motion Spell Checker — Help

Scanning

Pick a scope (Active Comp, Entire Project, Selected Layers, or Selected Comps) and click Scan. The checker reads layer names, text layers, marker comments, string effect parameters, and quoted string literals inside expressions.

Settings

Word matching (Ignore ALL-CAPS, Skip words with numbers), layer filters (hidden / locked), whether highlights show in the comp, and Verify Dictionary all live in Settings.

Keyboard shortcuts

Enter runs Scan (when a dropdown or overlay isn't focused), and Esc closes any open dialog. There's no separate Undo button — everything this panel does (replacing words, in particular) is a normal After Effects edit, so Cmd+Z (Mac) or Ctrl+Z (Windows) always undoes it.

Highlighting in the comp

After a scan, every layer with a spelling error gets a red outline directly in the Composition viewer, drawn on a non-rendering guide layer named "MSC Highlights" — it never modifies your actual layers and is excluded from render.

Fixing a word

Select a misspelled word from the list, review where it appears, then either pick a suggestion and click Replace (fixes every occurrence of that word), click Ignore (skips it for this session and saves it to ignoredWords.txt), or click + Dictionary to whitelist it permanently.

Dictionary files

Drop category word lists (one word per line, or wrong -> right correction lines) into the extension's Dictionary folder for full coverage beyond the built-in fallback list. Use Settings > Verify Dictionary to see what's currently loaded.
